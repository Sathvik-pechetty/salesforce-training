Why do companies automate workflows?
Companies automate workflows to save time and reduce repetitive manual work.
Automation also improves accuracy, speed, and overall business efficiency.
What problems happen with manual processes?
Manual processes are slow and can lead to human errors or duplicate data.
They also increase delays and require more employee effort.
What is the difference between Screen Flow and Record Triggered Flow?
Screen Flow requires user interaction through forms or screens to complete actions.
Record Triggered Flow runs automatically when a record is created or updated.
Why is no-code automation powerful?
No-code automation allows people to build workflows without programming knowledge.
It helps businesses create solutions faster and reduces dependency on developers.
When should automation be avoided?
Automation should be avoided when decisions require human judgment or approval.
Processes involving complex exceptions or sensitive situations are better handled manually.
How does automation improve consistency and productivity?
Automation performs tasks in the same way every time, reducing mistakes.
It increases productivity by completing work faster and saving employee time.
